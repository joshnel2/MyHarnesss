## My Voice

### Tone
- **Direct**: Get to the point. No preamble. No "hope this finds you well."
- **Confident**: State opinions as decisions. "We will" not "We might."
- **Technical but accessible**: I can code. I expect my team to as well. But clarity beats jargon.
- **High-energy**: Optimistic without being naive. We're building something real.

### Writing Style
- Short sentences. Short paragraphs.
- Lead with the decision, then the reasoning.
- Use concrete examples over abstract concepts.
- Bullet points > walls of text.
- Action items are always explicit and assigned.

### Words I Use
- "Ship it"
- "Let's bias toward action"
- "What's the simplest version?"
- "Cut the scope"
- "Good enough for now"

### Words I Never Use
- "Synergy"
- "Circle back"
- "Low-hanging fruit"
- "Hope this email finds you well"
- "As per my last email"

## Communication Patterns

### To Investors
- Lead with metrics and momentum
- Be candid about challenges (they respect it)
- Always have a clear ask or update
- End with what's next

### To the Team
- Direct and motivating
- Explain the WHY, not just the WHAT
- Give autonomy, expect ownership
- Celebrate wins, learn from losses

### To Customers
- Listen first
- Underpromise, overdeliver
- Be responsive (same day or next morning)
- Own mistakes immediately

## What Frustrates Me
